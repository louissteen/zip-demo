# Find your way around

The portal has a small number of pages. Most of your work happens on two of them.

## Home

Home shows what needs your attention: new purchase orders you have not acknowledged, invoices your customer has returned to you with a question, requests for updated documents, and anything else waiting on you.

If you only check one page, check this one. Everything that blocks a payment to you appears here first.

## Purchase orders

Every purchase order your customer has issued to you, with its number, date, amount, and how much of it has been invoiced so far. Open a PO to read the line items and terms, download a copy, and start an invoice against it.

See [View purchase orders](../orders-and-invoices/viewing-purchase-orders.md).

## Invoices

Every invoice you have submitted, with its current status. This is where you find out whether an invoice is being processed, has been queried, or has been approved for payment.

See [Submit an invoice](../orders-and-invoices/submitting-an-invoice.md) and [Check payment status](../orders-and-invoices/checking-payment-status.md).

## Payments

Payments your customer has issued to you, with the date, the amount, the method, and the invoices each payment covers. A payment covering several invoices lists all of them, which is what you need to apply cash correctly on your side.

## Company profile

Your company's details as your customer holds them: legal entity, addresses, tax forms, bank accounts, and contacts. This is where you keep information current and where you submit changes when something moves.

See [Update banking details](../company-profile/updating-banking-details.md) and [Manage contacts](../company-profile/managing-contacts.md).

## Sourcing events

If your customer has invited you to bid, events appear here with their deadlines. Open an event to read the requirements, ask clarifying questions, and submit your response before the deadline.

## Assessments

If your customer requires a security, privacy, or other assessment, the questionnaire appears here. Where you have already answered a question for this customer, your previous answer is prefilled for you to confirm or update, and uploading a current audit report or certification can remove a large part of the questionnaire.

## Switching customers

If several of your customers use Zip, the customer switcher at the top of the page moves between them. Each customer's orders, invoices, and payments are entirely separate.

{% hint style="info" %}
Notifications follow the contact roles set on your company profile. If the wrong person is getting purchase orders, or the right person is not getting remittance advice, fix it in **Contacts** rather than forwarding emails by hand.
{% endhint %}

## Getting help

Questions about a purchase order, an invoice, or a payment go to your customer. The buyer contact is on the purchase order, and you can add a comment on an invoice to ask a question that stays attached to the record.

Problems with the portal itself, such as sign-in trouble, are covered in the [Help Center](https://app.gitbook.com/s/xKEqt4qa9BH9Mk6NAbiK/).
