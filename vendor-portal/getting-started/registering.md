# Register for the portal

Your customer invites you to the portal by email. The invitation goes to the contact your customer has on file, which is often the person who handles your accounts receivable.

{% stepper %}
{% step %}
## Open the invitation

Find the invitation email from your customer and select the link. If you cannot find it, check your spam or quarantine folder. Invitations are time limited; if yours has expired, ask your customer contact to resend it.
{% endstep %}

{% step %}
## Create your account

Enter your name and set a password, or sign in with your work identity provider if your company uses single sign-on. Use your work email address, not a personal one, so the account transfers if you change roles.
{% endstep %}

{% step %}
## Confirm your email

Zip sends a confirmation code. Enter it to verify the address.
{% endstep %}

{% step %}
## Set up two-factor authentication

Two-factor authentication is required. Register an authenticator app and store the recovery codes somewhere safe and separate from your password. Your account controls where your company gets paid, which is why this step is not optional.
{% endstep %}

{% step %}
## Complete the onboarding form

Your customer's onboarding form asks for your legal entity details, your tax form, your bank details, and your contacts. Answer as your company's finance team would answer, not as the sales team would. Save and come back if you need to gather documents.
{% endstep %}

{% step %}
## Submit

Once you submit, your customer reviews the information. If anything needs correcting, you will get an email naming the specific field, and you correct it in the portal rather than starting again.
{% endstep %}
{% endstepper %}

{% hint style="warning" %}
Enter your bank details in the portal yourself. Do not send them by email to anyone, including someone who says they work for your customer. Payment redirection fraud almost always starts with an email asking for bank details outside the normal process.
{% endhint %}

## One account, several customers

If more than one of your customers uses Zip, you can add them to the same account. When a new customer invites you, sign in with your existing account rather than creating a second one, and the new customer appears in the customer switcher.

Each customer sees only its own relationship with you. Your other customers, and what you invoice them, are never visible.

## If you cannot sign in

Use **Forgot password** to reset your password. If you have lost your two-factor device and your recovery codes, contact your customer, who can verify your identity and start a reset.

<details>

<summary>Someone else at my company already registered us.</summary>

Ask them to invite you as an additional contact rather than registering separately. See [Manage contacts](../company-profile/managing-contacts.md).

</details>

<details>

<summary>Do I have to use the portal?</summary>

That is your customer's decision. Many customers will not process an invoice that does not come through the portal, and none will change bank details based on an email. Even where email invoicing is accepted, the portal is the only place you can see payment status yourself.

</details>
