# Submit an invoice

Submitting through the portal is the fastest route to payment, because the invoice arrives already matched to the purchase order instead of being read out of an email attachment.

{% stepper %}
{% step %}
## Start from the purchase order

Open the PO on the **Purchase orders** page and select **Create invoice**. Starting here means the PO number, entity, and currency are correct without you typing them.
{% endstep %}

{% step %}
## Enter your invoice details

Enter your own invoice number and invoice date. The invoice number must be unique across everything you have sent this customer. A repeated number is rejected as a suspected duplicate.
{% endstep %}

{% step %}
## Select the lines you are billing

Choose the PO lines this invoice covers and enter the quantity or amount for each. For a partial delivery or a monthly service, bill only what has been delivered in the period. The remaining balance stays available on the PO.
{% endstep %}

{% step %}
## Add tax and any additional charges

Enter tax as your invoice states it. Charges such as freight or duties can be added only if the PO provides for them; if it does not, ask the buyer for a revision before invoicing.
{% endstep %}

{% step %}
## Attach your invoice document

Upload your own invoice PDF. Most customers require it for their records even though you have entered the details, and tax authorities in many countries require the original document.
{% endstep %}

{% step %}
## Check the totals and submit

Confirm that the total on your document matches the total you entered. A mismatch is the single most common reason an invoice is returned.
{% endstep %}
{% endstepper %}

## Avoiding the usual rejections

Most rejected invoices fail for one of a few reasons.

**Wrong or missing PO number.** Every invoice needs the PO it bills against.

**Amount over the remaining PO balance.** Check the remaining balance before you invoice.

**Entity mismatch.** The entity on your invoice must match the entity on the PO.

**Description mismatch.** Use the PO's descriptions. An invoice line nobody can map to a PO line goes to a human, and humans are slower than matching.

**Duplicate invoice number.** Reusing a number, including on a corrected invoice, causes a duplicate rejection. Issue a new number.

{% hint style="info" %}
Submit as soon as you have delivered. Payment terms usually run from receipt of a valid invoice, not from delivery, so an invoice you send three weeks late is paid three weeks later.
{% endhint %}

## Other ways to send an invoice

Some customers also accept invoices by email to a dedicated address, or through an electronic invoicing network. Your customer's preferences are shown on the **Invoices** page. Whichever route you use, the PO number must be on the invoice.

The portal remains the only place you can see the status of what you sent.

## Corrections and credit notes

You cannot edit a submitted invoice. If you find an error, cancel it if it is still in early processing, or issue a credit note against it and submit a corrected invoice with a new number.

Credit notes are submitted the same way, referencing the original invoice. Your customer applies the credit against what it owes you, so a credit note reduces your next payment rather than triggering a refund.

## After submission

Your invoice appears on the **Invoices** page with a status you can follow. See [Check payment status](checking-payment-status.md).
