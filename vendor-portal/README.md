# Vendor Portal

The Zip vendor portal is where you manage your relationship with a customer who uses Zip. You complete onboarding, keep your company details current, view the purchase orders your customer has issued to you, submit invoices against them, and check when you will be paid.

The portal is free to you, and one account works across every customer that invites you. You do not need to install anything, and you do not need a separate login for each customer.
