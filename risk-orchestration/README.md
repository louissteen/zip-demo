# Risk Orchestration

Third-party risk assessment usually fails for a process reason rather than a technical one. Nobody tells the security team about a purchase until the contract is signed, and by then the assessment is a formality that delays a decision already made.

Risk Orchestration fixes the timing. Assessments are triggered from the intake request, from the answers the requester already gave, and they run in parallel with the rest of the approval process. Security, privacy, finance, and any other risk domain your organization runs get involved when the request is raised, not when it is done.
