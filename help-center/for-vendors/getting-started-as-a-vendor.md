# Getting started as a vendor

If a customer has asked you to complete onboarding in Zip, this article covers what to expect. There is no cost to you, and you do not need a Zip subscription.

## Registering

You will receive an email invitation from your customer. It contains a secure link to the Zip vendor portal.

{% stepper %}
{% step %}
## Open the invitation

Select the link in the invitation email. Links expire, so if yours has expired, ask your customer contact to resend it.
{% endstep %}

{% step %}
## Create your account

Set a password and confirm your email address. If your company already has a portal account with this customer, sign in instead of creating a second one.
{% endstep %}

{% step %}
## Complete the onboarding questionnaire

Provide your legal entity details, addresses and contacts. Your customer decides which questions are required.
{% endstep %}

{% step %}
## Submit tax and banking information

Upload the tax form your customer requests and enter your remittance bank details.
{% endstep %}
{% endstepper %}

## What your customer needs from you

* Legal entity name and registered address
* A remittance email address for payment notifications
* A tax form, usually a W-9 for US entities or the applicable W-8 series form for non-US entities
* Bank account details for payment
* Contacts for purchase orders, invoices and remittance

{% hint style="warning" %}
Your customer will verify bank details independently before paying you. Zip will never ask you to confirm or change banking details over the phone or by replying to an email.
{% endhint %}

## After onboarding

Once your customer approves your record, you can:

* Receive and download purchase orders
* Submit invoices against those purchase orders
* Track payment status and download remittance advice
* Keep your contacts and addresses current
