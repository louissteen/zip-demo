# Zip Academy

Zip Academy is where structured training lives. The help center answers a specific question; Academy teaches a role end to end.

## What is available

| Format | Description | Best for |
| ------ | ----------- | -------- |
| On-demand courses | Short recorded modules with knowledge checks | New users getting started |
| Live training | Scheduled instructor-led sessions | Teams rolling out a new module |
| Certification | Assessed tracks with a shareable credential | Administrators and power users |

## Learning tracks

* **Requester essentials**, covering raising a request and tracking it through approval
* **Approver essentials**, covering reviewing requests and bills
* **Accounts payable**, covering invoice coding, bill approval and payment runs
* **Administrator**, covering intake forms, workflows, permissions and integrations
* **Vendor management**, covering onboarding, risk and the vendor lifecycle

## Enrolling

Sign in at the Academy with the same credentials you use for Zip. If your organization has single sign-on enabled, you are signed in automatically.

{% hint style="info" %}
Certification credentials can be added to a LinkedIn profile once the assessed track is complete.
{% endhint %}
