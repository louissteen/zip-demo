# Approving a bill

When an invoice has been coded, Zip creates a bill and assigns it to the approvers your organization's policy requires. Approving a bill confirms that the goods or services were received and that the coding is correct.

<figure><img src="../images/bills-list.png" alt="The Bills page showing bills awaiting approval" width="820"></figure>

## Finding bills that need you

Open the **Bills** page. It has three tabs:

* **Needs approval** shows bills where you are a current approver
* **Pending AP action** shows bills waiting on the accounts payable team
* **All** shows every bill you have permission to see

Within **Needs approval**, **My approvals** holds your queue and **Others' approvals** shows what colleagues are working through.

## Reviewing a bill

{% stepper %}
{% step %}
## Open the bill

Select the invoice number to open the bill alongside the original invoice document.
{% endstep %}

{% step %}
## Check the PO match

Confirm the bill is matched to the right purchase order and that the remaining balance looks correct. An unmatched bill needs attention before it can be approved.
{% endstep %}

{% step %}
## Verify coding

Check the subsidiary, department, category and GL account. Correct anything wrong before approving rather than after.
{% endstep %}

{% step %}
## Approve or reject

Select **Approve** to pass the bill to the next approver, or **Reject** with a reason to send it back to accounts payable.
{% endstep %}
{% endstepper %}

## Filtering your queue

The filter row supports subsidiary, category, amount, dates, currency, department, PO match state, assignee, approver and flagged status. Save a filter combination as a view if you use it regularly.

{% hint style="info" %}
Sorting by **Due date** ascending is the fastest way to protect early payment discounts and avoid late fees.
{% endhint %}

## Priority and flags

Bills carry a priority of **Normal** or **Urgent**. Accounts payable sets priority, usually because a payment deadline is close. The flag icon marks a bill for follow-up without changing its priority or its place in the queue.

## Delegating while you are away

Set a delegate under **Settings**, then **Notifications**, then **Delegation**. Bills assigned to you during the delegation window go to your delegate, and the delegation is recorded on each bill's timeline.

{% hint style="warning" %}
Delegation applies to bills assigned after the delegation starts. Bills already in your queue stay with you, so clear them or reassign them before you go.
{% endhint %}
