# AI Procurement Concierge

AI Procurement Concierge is the conversational assistant that sits in front of your procurement process. Requesters ask it a question in plain language, in Zip or in Slack or Teams, and it answers using your policies, your catalog, your vendor records, and the status of their own requests.

The goal is to reduce the number of questions that reach your procurement inbox. Most of what a requester needs to know is already written down somewhere in Zip. The Concierge finds it, answers in context, and starts the right intake form when the answer is "raise a request".

Because the Concierge only reads what the requester is already permitted to see, it does not become a way around your access model. Governance, data boundaries, and audit for every AI feature in Zip are described in [Governed AI](https://app.gitbook.com/s/oovoef3hZyAQg9DepDDH/).
