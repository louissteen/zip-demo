# Supplier Onboarding

Supplier Onboarding is how a company you want to buy from becomes a vendor you can pay. The vendor enters its own details in the vendor portal, uploads its tax form, and provides banking information. Zip validates what it can automatically, routes the rest to the teams that need to review it, and creates the vendor record in Zip and in your ERP once everything clears.

The work is deliberately pushed to the vendor. Vendors know their own tax classification and bank details, and every field they enter themselves is a field your AP team does not have to key from an email attachment.
