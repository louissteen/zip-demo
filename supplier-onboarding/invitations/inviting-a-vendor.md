# Invite a vendor

Onboarding starts with an invitation to a named contact at the vendor. Most invitations are generated automatically when a requester selects a vendor that does not exist yet, but you can also start one directly.

{% stepper %}
{% step %}
## Check the vendor does not already exist

Search on the **Vendors** page by name and by tax ID before inviting. Vendors trade under names that do not match their legal entity, and a duplicate created here is expensive to unwind later. See [Vendor Management](https://app.gitbook.com/s/VzFAfjeuJK2DKd9GcLgy/) for how matching works.
{% endstep %}

{% step %}
## Start the onboarding

Select **Onboard vendor**. Enter the vendor's legal name, the country of the entity you will contract with, and the subsidiary of yours that will buy from them.
{% endstep %}

{% step %}
## Name the vendor contact

Enter the contact who will complete onboarding, with their email. This should be someone in the vendor's finance or accounts receivable function, not the sales representative. The sales rep will forward it, which adds days and often loses it.
{% endstep %}

{% step %}
## Assign the internal owner

Set the person on your side who owns this onboarding, usually the requester or the category buyer. They are notified about stalls and they are who the vendor's questions come back to.
{% endstep %}

{% step %}
## Send the invitation

Select **Send invitation**. The vendor receives an email with a link to the [vendor portal](https://app.gitbook.com/s/6BeW2bup2FUvVioEI5Le/), where it registers and completes the onboarding form.
{% endstep %}
{% endstepper %}

{% hint style="warning" %}
Never accept banking details sent to you by email during onboarding, even from a contact you recognize, and even if they say the portal is not working. Redirecting a payment by impersonating a vendor contact is the most common fraud in accounts payable. Bank details enter through the portal or not at all.
{% endhint %}

## Tracking invitations

The onboarding pipeline shows every vendor in progress and where each one is: invited, registered, in progress, submitted, in review, or activated. It also shows how long each has been sitting.

Zip sends reminders on a schedule to vendors that have not completed, and notifies the internal owner when a vendor stalls past a threshold. Most stalls are not the vendor ignoring you; they are the invitation sitting with someone who cannot answer the questions.

## Reassigning the contact

If the invitation went to the wrong person, select **Change contact** on the onboarding record and enter the correct email. The original link is invalidated and a new invitation is sent. Anything already submitted is retained.

<details>

<summary>The vendor says it never received the invitation.</summary>

Check the delivery status on the onboarding record first. Invitations are most often blocked by the vendor's own mail filtering. Ask the vendor to allow your Zip sending domain, then resend. Do not send the vendor a copy of the form by email as a workaround, because submissions outside the portal are not verified and cannot be activated.

</details>

<details>

<summary>Can a vendor start onboarding without an invitation?</summary>

Your administrators can publish a self-registration link, for example on a supplier information page on your website. Self-registered vendors enter the same pipeline but are held for an additional internal check before review, since nobody on your side has vouched for them.

</details>
