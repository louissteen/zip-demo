# AI Contract Orchestration

Contracts are where procurement work becomes binding, and they are usually where it slows down. AI Contract Orchestration handles the whole path: a business user requests a contract from the same intake they use for everything else, Zip reads the document and pulls out the terms that matter, legal reviews the positions that actually deviate from your standards, and the executed agreement lands in a repository where its dates and obligations are tracked rather than forgotten.

The point is not to replace legal judgment. It is to make sure legal spends its time on the clauses that are genuinely off-standard instead of reading a familiar agreement end to end to find them.
